## How this site works

Everything here is written in Markdown and lives in
[`Pratyay360/docs`](https://github.com/Pratyay360/docs) on GitHub. Pages are
published with astro Starlight and served from [docs.lightcms.me](https://docs.lightcms.me).

- Found a mistake? Use the **Edit** link at the bottom of any page.
- Looking for something specific? Press <kbd>/</kbd> to search.
- Prefer a quick start? Begin with [Getting Started](getting-started/).
